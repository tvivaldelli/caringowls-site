How it works

Three simple steps to protect your loved one with as little disruption to their routine as possible.

1

Keep their number

We give your family a private Caring Owls line. One dial code forwards your loved one's calls to it. Their number doesn't change, nobody has to be told anything, and turning it off is one more dial code. Want text protection too? Full Protection moves their number to us permanently.

2

Set up approved contacts

Family members add trusted contacts through a simple web dashboard. Only approved people can call your loved one. On Full Protection, texts are filtered the same way.

3

Unknown callers go to you, not them

Anyone not on the approved list reaches a voicemail your family reviews from the dashboard. Approve a caller once and they ring through from then on. Your loved one never hears the scam call.

Questions families ask

Should I take away her phone?

Most families who've been through this say no — it's her last link to the outside world. The answer is usually to limit who can reach her and who she can reach, without taking the phone. That's what Caring Owls does, and you manage it from your own phone.

Can't I just set up her iPhone to only allow contacts?

You can, and for some families that's enough. What you can't do from her Settings is see who tried to call, approve a new caller from your own phone, get alerted when she dials a stranger, or review a voicemail before deciding. Caring Owls is that layer.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Caring Owls runs on iPhone. The caregiver dashboard works in any browser.

Will my mom notice anything changed?

On Quick Start, she keeps her number and her phone. Calls from people you've approved arrive like any normal call. She'll have a new app on her phone that she rarely needs to open.

Can we undo it?

Quick Start turns off with one dial code, and she's back to exactly how things were. Full Protection involves moving her number, which is reversible but takes time.

What about scam texts?

Full Protection filters texts and lets your family review unknown senders. Quick Start relies on the iPhone's built-in Filter Unknown Senders, which hides stranger texts but doesn't show them to you.

What if she finds the regular Phone app?

It's still on the phone, moved out of sight. A determined person can find it. Caring Owls makes the protected path the easy one; it doesn't lock the phone down. If you need a fully locked-down phone, we'll tell you honestly that we're not the right fit.
